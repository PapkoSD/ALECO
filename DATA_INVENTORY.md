# Data inventory: Slovak local food hub

Built 2026-11-19, extended to all of Slovakia the same evening, hand-checked on 2026-11-20. Everything is reproducible with the scripts in `scripts/` (commands in `README.md`). `scripts/build_seed.py` rewrites `data/out/report.md` with the current counts on every run.

## Hand-check (2026-11-20)

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Every website on the map tested | 54 dead, parked or broken links removed (domain gone, expired certificate, connection refused); 8 deep links that moved replaced by the home page |
| Duplicates | 34 OSM entries mapped twice removed; 13 certified producers merged into the OSM place where they sell; 21 groups of pins at the same spot spread out so each can be clicked |
| Out-of-date OSM entries | 204 places last edited in 2019 or earlier; the detail card now says so |
| 218 certified producers looked up online | **14 verified as selling directly** (farm shop hours, e-shop, pick-up): shown with a "Predaj" line and an "overené" mark. **28 taken off the map**: 14 wholesale-only (supermarket private label, industrial) and 14 not local food (supplements, cosmetics, imported raw materials). 19 more got a website or phone |
| Left for phone calls | **175 producers** the web could not settle, 18 of them with a phone number already: `data/out/call_list.csv` (private), Bratislava region first |

Curated results: `data/checks/places_checks.csv` (committed). Generated by scripts: `data/checks/websites.csv`, `data/checks/web_discovery.csv`.

## Known gaps and caveats

- **A certificate does not mean direct sales.** The detail card says so. Hand-checking and producer outreach are what turn a pin into a reliable listing.
- **Farm shops are under-mapped in OSM** (99 nationwide). Our own add/fix form and adding checked places back to OSM will matter more than any new dataset.
- **103 certified producers sit at the village centre or postcode area**, because their certificate address has no street. They are drawn lighter on the map and labelled as approximate.
- **Bakeries are over half the OSM places.** They are independent bakeries, but they can crowd out farms in some towns, so the type chips matter.
- **The base map (OpenFreeMap) was slow during testing**, taking 10–15 s at high zoom. If it stays slow, self-hosting a Slovakia PMTiles file on the same static host is the fix.
- **GDPR:** sole traders are never published from registers; they appear only after they agree.
